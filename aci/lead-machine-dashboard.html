07 — The Fix: What's Being Built
Solution Stack — In Progress
🔨
New Website
Rebuilt to qualify intent before form submission. Reduces bad lead volume (currently 39.5% YTD), improves CPL efficiency, and strengthens SEO-driven traffic quality at the source.
🔨
SMS Automation — Sub-5 Minute Response
Automated first-touch SMS within 60 seconds of lead creation. Directly targets the 10.7–13.2 hour response gap on Meta and Google leads. Highest leverage single fix available.
🔨
Call Center Integration
Dedicated admissions calling workflow with lead routing, structured follow-up cadence, and tour no-show recovery. Solves the capacity constraint — team cannot handle current lead volume, this is not a motivation problem.
🔨
Email Nurture Sequences
Program-specific drips for every funnel stage. Tour no-show recovery, FA appointment reminders, re-engagement for leads sitting at New Lead for 7+ days.
🔨
Google Conversion Loop — GCLID + Offline Conversions
Implementing GCLID tracking, Tag Assistant verification, and offline conversion imports to feed actual enrollment data back to Google Ads. Google will optimize campaigns against real enrollments — not lead form fills. Closes the loop between ad spend and revenue, and fundamentally changes how Google allocates budget across programs and audiences.
