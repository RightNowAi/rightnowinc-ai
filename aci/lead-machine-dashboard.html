07 — The Fix · Solution Stack & Projected Impact
Solution Stack
Ranked by leverage · implementation status
1
Speed to Lead: Sub-5-Minute Protocol
YTD 33.4% hit under 5 min. October dropped to 24.1%. Every 10-point improvement in S2L <5min drives ~+1.5% CVR at current volume. Target: 50%+. Action: dedicated first-response role or automated SMS bridge with human handoff within 5 min of lead arrival.
2
4–24 Hour Bucket Rescue
30.2% YTD, spiked to 38.6% in October — the single biggest untapped pool. A same-day automated follow-up sequence (SMS + email at 2hr, 6hr, 12hr) targeting this bucket could convert an additional 8–12 enrollments per month at zero additional spend.
3
No-Call Elimination
619 YTD leads (5.9%) received zero contact. October trending to 8.7%. At 5.10% CVR, 619 uncalled leads = ~32 missed enrollments = $512K uncollected tuition YTD. Zero-tolerance target: same-day dialer follow-up on every new lead, no exceptions.
4
Unable to Reach Re-Engagement
1,515 leads (14.4%) sit in "Unable to Reach" — not disqualified, just untouched. A structured 3-touch sequence (call + SMS + email over 5 days) at 3–5% CVR = 45–75 additional enrollments. One-time sweep. No new spend.
5
September Google Spend Audit
September Google spend jumped to $50,168 (vs $35–40K avg prior months) but produced only 87 Google leads — $576/lead. Meta dropped to $4,800. Channel mix shifted dramatically. Audit September Google campaigns for waste before November budget allocation.
