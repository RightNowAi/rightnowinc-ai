01

Today vs. the new site

The site is not getting dramatically bigger. It is getting reorganized around the questions people actually ask before they enroll.

Exists today To be built

Three things to take away

  • Four questions have no home on the site right now. How to enroll, what it costs, when classes start, and which campus. Every competitor in the category answers all four.
  • Nothing gets lost. The certification, employment, and financial aid detail currently spread across twelve separate pages moves onto the program pages and into one tuition section. Same information, fewer clicks.
  • There are no ad landing pages today. Every dollar of advertising currently lands on a page built for browsing, with full navigation and dozens of ways to leave.

Four decisions worth naming

DecisionWhy
“Tuition Planning,” not “Financial Aid”Planning implies help is available. Aid implies you can’t afford it. Softer entry to the single scariest topic on the site.
Program GuideA downloadable guide gives someone a way to raise their hand without committing to a phone call. There is currently nothing between reading a page and handing over a phone number.
Careers at ACITwo campuses and growing. Trade instructors are hard to hire, and a real careers page is a recruiting asset.
Student Area moves to the footerIt serves current students, not prospective ones. Keeping it in the main navigation splits the path for someone still deciding.

Austin Trade Careers needs compliance review before it is built. The owner has raised the idea of posting local trade jobs on the site. As proposed here, the page is editorial — what the Austin trade market looks like, using published third-party data — with no live job listings and no salary figures.

A live job board is a different product with different exposure: listing wages next to an enroll button can be read as an implied earnings or placement claim even when no claim is made. That version should not be scoped until ACI’s compliance review has weighed in.

What has to happen

MoveWhatWhy
RemovePhlebotomy page and all referencesProgram discontinued.

BuildAdmissions, Tuition Planning, Campuses, Career Services, AboutThe five sections that answer pre-enrollment questions.
BuildFour ad landing pages plus templateSo advertising stops landing on browsing pages.
PreserveExisting page addressesEvery retired page redirects to its replacement so search rankings and existing links carry over.

Open items

  • Austin Trade Careers — needs compliance review before scope is set.
  • Spanish — staying at the two pages that exist today. The address structure will be built so a fuller Spanish section is a content project later, not another rebuild.
  • Content ownership — roughly 25 new pages need copy. Who writes and who approves needs a name against it before the build starts.
