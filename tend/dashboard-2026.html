Observations — June 1, 2026

✓ Positive

Blended CAC improved month-over-month

CAC dropped from $256.86 to $208.50 — a 19% improvement. Groups are being created faster than spend is increasing, which is the right direction. This is the metric that matters most to unit economics.

✓ Positive

Meta is the volume engine — and holding efficiency

557 of 931 registrations (59.8%) came from Meta at $17.89 CPL. Cold traffic converting at this rate is strong. The 6.1% reg→group rate is lower than other channels but expected from cold audiences — the volume more than compensates.

✓ Positive

Organic conversion quality remains the best

Organic/direct holds a $105 CAC — 3.6× cheaper than Google, 2.8× cheaper than Meta. At 8.2% reg→group, organic visitors are also more likely to follow through. SEO investment continues to compound.

⚑ Watch

Google CAC climbed to $379 — highest of all channels

Spend increased to $4,923 but groups created held at 13, pushing CAC up from $323 to $379. The 9.2% reg→group rate is the best of any paid channel, meaning intent is there — but CPL at $34.92 is the drag. Worth evaluating bid strategy and campaign mix.

⚑ Watch

4 group emails don't match any registration record

81 groups total, but 4 group creators have no corresponding registration email. Likely direct/offline sign-ups or data entry variance. Worth auditing the intake flow to ensure attribution is clean — these may be untracked organic conversions.

→ Context

Pace slowed vs prior period — still well ahead of goal

Prior report showed 261 reg/month. Current pace is 186/month. This is partly a math artifact — earlier months had high spikes. At 186/month we still need only 81/month for CY2026 goal. No corrective action needed, but worth watching if pace dips further.
