Observations — November 3, 2026

✓ Milestone

Registration goal exceeded at 165%

2,474 registrations against a 1,500 target, projecting to ~196% by year end. Registration has decisively stopped being the constraint — the program has more interested people than it can currently activate.

✓ Steady

Organic remains the efficiency leader

$188 CAC — roughly half of either paid channel — at the strongest reg→group rate (6.5%). With paid spend lighter, organic is doing quiet, compounding work on both volume and quality.

→ Next focus

Group activation is the priority lever

Groups at 108 (72% of goal), pacing toward ~128 EOY. The gap between 2,474 registrations and 108 groups is the opportunity — the webinar cohort and the broader registered-not-started pool are the target of current reactivation work.

→ Test

Pastor Traffic opened a new audience

A Q4 prospecting test reached the pastor/decision-maker persona at $0.19 per landing-page view — cheap top-of-funnel. Registrations and groups from this audience are still maturing; kept out of core CAC until attributable.

⚑ Watch

Re-engagement retarget drove clicks, not groups

The registered-no-group retarget produced landing-page views but few group starts — a signal the conversion barrier sits downstream of the click (the group-creation flow itself), not in reach or awareness.

⚑ Watch

Google CAC highest at $432; KWsearch still capped

Solid traffic but a $23.47 CPL and slowed group conversion keep CAC high. KWsearch — the efficient Search converter — remains budget-limited at $19/day while spend flows to PerfMax. Uncapping it is the cleanest efficiency gain available.
