Observations — July 6, 2026

✓ Improved

CY2026 registration goal is nearly locked

1,345 registrations — 90% of the 1,500 annual target with almost 6 months to go. Only 26/month needed to close it; running 220/month. On pace to clear the goal in mid-summer and project to 176% by year end.

✓ Improved

June closed at the lowest CAC yet — $184

The full-month blended CAC bottomed in June even as spend stayed high. Spend scaled ~20% over the period while blended CAC held at $248 — output is keeping pace with investment.

✓ Steady

Organic stays the efficiency leader

$130 CAC — 2.1× cheaper than Meta, 2.5× cheaper than Google — at a healthy 8.2% reg→group. SEO and word-of-mouth continue to compound with no added spend beyond retainer.

⚑ Watch

Meta reg→group dipped to 6.7%

Down from 7.1% as an 834-registration surge outpaced group creation. Likely a timing gap — newest registrations haven't converted yet — but worth confirming next period that the cohort catches up rather than stalls.

⚑ Watch

Google CAC remains highest at $325

Best paid reg→group rate (9.2%) but CPL of $29.82 keeps CAC elevated. KWsearch continues to convert well while sitting budget-capped — headroom exists to shift spend toward the efficient campaign.
