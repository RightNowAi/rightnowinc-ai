Retargeting the unconverted pool delivered registrations at $1.89 — the cheapest of any source

The August webinar is a co-registration: every webinar signup also registers for Tend, so all 175 net-new signups count toward the program total. Retargeting previously-unconverted contacts produced registrations at $1.89 each vs $9.21 for cold — proof the existing audience is the highest-ROI pool. Because the webinar trains attendees to start and run a group, this cohort is expected to convert to groups at an above-average rate as the training completes. 8 additional webinar registrants were already in the Tend list and are counted once, not twice.

Observations — July 14, 2026

✓ Milestone

CY2026 registration goal reached — 6 months early

1,535 registrations clears the 1,500 annual target. Projects to 192% by year end. The August webinar co-registration added 175 net-new in a single push, tipping the program over the line ahead of schedule.

✓ Standout

Webinar retargeting hit $1.89 per registration

Retargeting the unconverted pool produced registrations at a fraction of cold-traffic cost ($9.21). Cheapest acquisition of any source to date and a clear signal to keep working the existing audience.

✓ Steady

Organic remains the efficiency leader

$130 CAC — 2.3× cheaper than Meta, 2.4× cheaper than Google — at 7.9% reg→group. Meta's blended CPL also fell to $16.52 as the low-cost webinar volume came in.

→ Context

Meta reg→group reads 5.5% — understated by design

175 fresh webinar registrations entered the denominator with no time yet to convert. Since the webinar actively trains attendees to start a group, this cohort should convert above average as it completes — expect the rate to climb next period.

⚑ Watch

Retargeting isn't attributing on the main registration flow

The webinar's retargeting tracks cleanly, but only 1 rt_traffic registration appears in the main flow — the always-on retargeting budget is pointed at group-starts, not registration capture, so it under-reports there. Worth aligning tracking if registration credit is wanted.

⚑ Watch

Google CAC highest at $317; KWsearch still capped

Best paid reg→group (9.0%) but CPL of $28.41 keeps CAC elevated. KWsearch — the efficient Search converter — remains budget-limited while spend leaks to weaker campaigns. Uncapping it is the cleanest efficiency gain available.
