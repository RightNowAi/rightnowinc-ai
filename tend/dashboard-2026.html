Observations — August 9, 2026

✓ Milestone

CY2026 registration goal exceeded — 150% of target

2,245 registrations against a 1,500 goal, five months early. The August webinar drove it, adding 770 net-new co-registrations. Registration is decisively no longer the constraint — group conversion is.

✓ Standout

Webinar delivered registrations at $7.09 — cheapest of the year

770 net-new at $7.09 blended CPL, less than half the cost of standard Meta cold traffic. Retargeting the unconverted pool was cheapest at $5.98. The co-registration mechanic is the most efficient acquisition run to date.

→ Context

Meta reg→group at 3.5% and CAC at $376 — both temporary

The 770 webinar registrations sit in Meta's numerator with zero groups because the event runs in August. This mechanically depresses conversion and inflates CAC. Both recover sharply once the cohort creates groups — expect a large swing next period.

⚑ Watch

Group creation is now the core lever

104 groups (69% of goal) against 2,245 registrations means a large activation pool. With registration goal cleared, the highest-value work shifts to converting the ~2,100 registered-but-not-started — the webinar cohort chief among them.

→ Data hygiene

44 international registrations identified and excluded

A targeting misconfiguration on the webinar campaign let in ~44 non-US registrations (Nigeria, Kenya, Philippines and others). Identified via the state field and removed from all counts. US-only figures are reported throughout.

⚑ Watch

Google CAC $344; KWsearch still budget-capped

Solid 7.4% reg→group but a $25.40 CPL keeps CAC high. KWsearch — the efficient Search converter — remains limited by budget while spend flows to weaker campaigns. Uncapping it stays the cleanest available efficiency gain.
