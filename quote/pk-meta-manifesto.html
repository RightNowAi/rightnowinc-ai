The premise

Nobody books a $56,000 week from a cold Meta ad.

That's the entire lesson from every failed luxury campaign on Meta. Cold audiences don't convert at this price point. They never did. The people who tried it learned the hard way - running cold ads to a unqualified audience.

Meta knows more about your next guest's spending behavior than the IRS does. Where they vacation. What they spend. When they start dreaming about their next trip. We're not buying ads. We're borrowing the most sophisticated behavioral database ever built — and pointing it at exactly the right people, with exactly the right property, at exactly the right moment.

What Meta actually does — when used correctly — is something far more valuable. It finds your warmest prospects and stays in front of them until they're ready to book. It's not a fishing pole. It's a net you've already cast.

Every guest who visits purekauai.com and leaves without booking is a warm lead walking out the door. Meta is how we bring them back.

The plays
01
Start with people who already love us
Upload the guest list. The highest-intent, lowest-cost audience on the platform. Past guests rebook. Past guests refer. This is the fastest proof of concept — and the foundation for growing our following and view counts with an audience that already has a reason to care.
02
Clone our best guests
Meta's algorithm finds people who look exactly like the guests on our list — same wealth signals, same travel behavior, same zip codes. One percent lookalike. Tight, qualified, ready.
03
Follow the serious ones home
Anyone who viewed a property listing, watched the brand film past the halfway mark, or landed on the booking page without completing — they see Pure Kauai again. Dynamically. On the exact villa they were considering. Multiple times during the mid-funnel window .
04
Open the advisor channel
Luxury travel advisors book $100,000+ trips for clients they trust completely. We use our list and clone it, reach them personally, and invite them into the Pure Kauai ohana. Get them on our email list — build the relationship on an owned platform forever.
05
Surgical cold, not scatter
HNW zip codes with direct flights to Kauai. Known private aviation FBO locations. The narrowest possible cold audience — people with the means and the access to book tomorrow if they want to.
On the advisor play
The fam trip cta opt-in is not an event. It's a list-building bridge. Every advisor who interacts with Pure Kauai becomes a direct email contact — a relationship we own and compound over time, independent of any platform.
